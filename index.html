Legal Terms & CNT Act Advisory

1. Platform Nature

JAMINDAR LAND BAZAAR is an online informational discovery marketplace. The platform is not a government body, registrar, or legal title insurer.

2. Chota Nagpur Tenancy (CNT) Act Compliance

In accordance with the CNT Act (Chota Nagpur Tenancy Act, 1908), properties marked as CNT Land are subject to strict regulatory transfer restrictions in Jharkhand. Buyers are exclusively responsible for ensuring compliance with section restrictions, district commissioner permissions, and category eligibility before entering into any transaction.

3. Buyer's Due Diligence

Buyers must independently inspect Khatiyan, Jamabandi, Land Mutation receipts (झारभूमि / Jharbhoomi portal), Non-Encumbrance Certificates, and registry archives before issuing any financial consideration.
